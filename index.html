About

About me

I completed my BS in Electrical Engineering (electronics stream) at FAST-NUCES Karachi in 2023. Since 2024 I have worked as an instructor in the Department of Computer Science at FAST-NUCES, where I run undergraduate labs and write lab manuals and assessments.

My final-year group project detected fire with a camera on a Raspberry Pi and used servo motors to aim a water nozzle at it. My recent projects use public data and open code to study energy problems that matter in Pakistan, such as load-shedding, hot summers and the growth of electric two-wheelers.

Projects

Projects

Self-directed simulation projects built on public data. Each one has its code, data and results on GitHub, so every number can be reproduced, and two have a written technical report. None of them is a published paper.

Power systems · e-mobility

AC load, e-scooter charging and transformer ageing in Lahore

Measured data from 34 Lahore homes show the summer feeder peaking near midnight. The charging rule changes yearly transformer ageing from +8 % to +36 % for the same energy.

Power electronics · reliability

Climate or design? E-scooter controllers in five cities

A thermal model of a MOSFET controller over a year of weather. Hot cities add 1.1 to 2.6 times the damage of Madrid; a box with no airflow raises it about 17 times.

Energy management · storage

Battery reserve for rooftop PV under load-shedding, Karachi

A rule that uses the published load-shedding timetable serves 99.7 % of outage energy with 23 % less grid import than the best fixed reserve.

Solar · embedded

How many ADC bits does MPPT need?

In full sun a few bits are enough; in low light 10 bits work down to about 100 W/m².

Final-year project · 2023 · team of three

AI-based fire detection and suppression on a Raspberry Pi

A Raspberry Pi 4 with an 8 MP camera runs a TensorFlow Lite detector trained on 5,599 indoor images of fire and light that we recorded and labelled ourselves. When it finds fire, servo motors aim a water nozzle and a relay starts a 12 V pump.

Raspberry Pi 4TensorFlow Lite5,599 own images
